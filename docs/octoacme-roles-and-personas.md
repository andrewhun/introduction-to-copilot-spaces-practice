# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies, ensure acceptance criteria are met, and validate that features deliver customer value with minimal defects.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach for each release
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Conduct manual QA when needed and collaborate with developers on test automation
- Identify and document quality gaps and risks
- Validate releases before deployment to production

### Goals
- Ensure releases meet quality standards and customer expectations
- Reduce production defects and support burden
- Enable rapid, confident delivery

### Typical Communication
- Quality reviews in sprint planning and retrospectives
- Test status updates in weekly syncs
- Defect triage and prioritization with the team

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborates on test automation strategies and defect resolution
- **Product Managers**: Aligns on acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- **Project Managers**: Updates on QA progress, blockers, and release readiness

---

## Technical Architect/Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define technical vision, review system designs, and ensure implementations are scalable, secure, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical strategy and architectural decisions
- Review designs and code for scalability, performance, and security
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor developers and guide technical best practices
- Ensure integration points are well-designed and documented

### Goals
- Deliver systems that scale and perform reliably
- Reduce technical debt and maintenance burden
- Enable rapid feature development through good design

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and RFC discussions
- Architecture guidance in planning and execution phases
- Risk escalation and dependency management

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provides technical guidance, reviews designs, and mentors on best practices
- **Product Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility and trade-offs during prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Escalates technical risks and dependencies affecting schedules

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile practices and self-organization
- Track sprint metrics and facilitate process improvements
- Shield the team from external distractions and scope creep

### Goals
- Maintain team velocity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and collaborative culture
- Enable continuous learning and process improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching conversations
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Coaches on agile practices and removes blockers
- **Product Managers**: Supports backlog refinement and sprint planning
- **Project Managers**: Collaborates on schedule management and risk tracking

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience, design interfaces, and validate usability to ensure features are intuitive and meet user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and create user personas and workflows
- Design interfaces and user experiences
- Create prototypes and design specifications
- Validate designs through user testing and feedback
- Ensure consistency across product surfaces and accessibility standards

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, delightful user experiences
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce support burden through good design

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specifications and component libraries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborates on implementation feasibility and component development
- **Product Managers**: Partners on customer insights and feature prioritization
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Works together on usability testing and acceptance criteria

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers design and maintain deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and operational processes to enable reliable, efficient releases.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure, databases, and deployment environments
- Implement monitoring, logging, and observability
- Enable fast, safe rollbacks and incident response
- Support security scanning and compliance requirements

### Goals
- Enable frequent, reliable releases with minimal manual effort
- Maintain system performance, availability, and security
- Reduce mean time to detect (MTTD) and mean time to recovery (MTTR) for incidents

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and release coordination
- Infrastructure and CI/CD updates in weekly syncs
- Incident response and post-mortems

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provides CI/CD infrastructure and supports production operations
- **Project Managers**: Coordinates deployment windows and release schedules
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Supports test environment setup and smoke test execution

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, approvals, and resource support to ensure project success and alignment with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Provide strategic direction and priority guidance
- Approve scope, timelines, and resource allocation
- Remove organizational blockers and escalate issues
- Provide feedback and validation on deliverables

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Enable teams to deliver with adequate resources and support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Decision gates and approval meetings
- Escalation and resource allocation discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Aligns on business objectives and prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Receives status updates and approves key decisions
- **Developers**: Provides business context and validation on deliverables

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Reference these roles when documenting responsibilities in project charters, risk registers, and communication plans.
